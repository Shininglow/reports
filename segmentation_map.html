Strategic reading — what the data signals

Biggest audience, lowest urgency

The Stuck & Drifting segment is 54% of the market but has chronic, diffuse pain — they feel it but aren't in crisis. Messaging needs to make the status quo feel more painful than the change. Loss aversion framing works here.

Most emotionally engaged

Lost Purpose posts spark 73 comments on average — the highest of any segment. These men are searching for identity, not tactics. They respond to frameworks that help them understand who they want to become, not checklists.

Rising keyword signal

Discipline Breakdown is the most solution-oriented segment — they know the problem and are actively looking for systems. Keywords like "morning routine" (90K/mo), "how to be disciplined" (14.8K) are stable/rising. Highest buyer intent after Active Builders.

Best conversion trigger

Post-Setback men are in crisis and ready to change now. Despite being only 4% of posts, they generate 64 avg comments — second highest. A life event (divorce, 30th birthday, job loss) has already done the convincing. They just need the path.
